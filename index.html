FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why should I have my trash bins professionally cleaned?
Trash bins can quickly accumulate foul odors. Cleaning your bins eliminates these odors and up to 99% of the bacteria and germs lurking inside. Additionally, sanitizing and deodorizing them can keep pests away and extend the life of your bins, preventing buildup and wear. The whole process is easy with simple sign-up, scheduling, reminders, and billing. You get clean bins with no hassle.
How often should I get my bins cleaned?
We recommend monthly cleaning, but this can vary depending on your trash volume and household size.
Do I need to empty my bins before you arrive?
Yes, we need empty garbage cans to clean. We schedule your stops to arrive the day after trash pickup to help minimize any garbage in your cans. If you have to put garbage in your cans before cleaning, please bag the trash and we will remove it and put it back.
How do you clean my bins?
We use a 200-degree high pressure system that kills 99% of bacteria inside of the bin, and then we blast the entire outside of the bin leaving it clean and sanitized, after spraying it with a deodorizer.
Why not just buy a new can from the city?
You could — but most cities only let you swap one can per year, and it typically costs about $50. And a brand-new can starts collecting the same grime on day one. For less than the price of that single swap, our quarterly plan deep-cleans and sanitizes your bins four times a year — so they stay fresh all year long, not just the week the new can shows up.
Do I need to keep my payment info on file?
Yes, you will need to enter your payment info on a secure payment portal for a recurring service.
Do you service my area?
Our service areas: Salem, Keizer, Turner, Aumsville, Sublimity, Stayton, Jefferson, Millersburg, Albany, and Woodburn. If you are not in a city we service but would like to see if we can clean your garbage cans, please contact us.
